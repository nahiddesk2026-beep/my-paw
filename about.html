Our Story

That's My Paw started at our kitchen table after one too many late nights scrolling through thousands of confusing pet product listings. Whether you are welcoming a playful puppy or spoiling a picky senior cat, finding gear that actually lasts shouldn't feel overwhelming.

Guided by our simple motto—Love • Care • Share—we spend hours researching materials, safety standards, washability, and real-world owner feedback across the US and UK so you can skip the guesswork.
